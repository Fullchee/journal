# Goals carry an optional Target, shown as progress only

A Goal may hold one Target: a measure (summed Habit times or minutes, or a Signal), an optional number, a minimum or limit, and a weekly, monthly or deadline period. This replaces "directional, not SMART" Goals, a Signal's separate target and the Pillar bar's own calculation, so numbers live in one place. Frequency stays the Habit's schedule. Behaviour-change science calls every level a goal and treats a target as a goal's measurable part ([goal terminology](../research/goal-terminology.md)). It also finds rigid numbers cost mood and letting go predicts wellbeing, so a Target never grades. It shows progress, never red or fail, never touches a Streak, Strength or History hit, and the Weekly review offers Keep, Adjust or Let go when a period ends short.

## Considered options

- **Keep Goals ungraded, numbers elsewhere**: numbers would stay in three places, and a deadline goal like "run 80 km by December" would have no home.
- **Weekly and monthly targets as Habit fields** (HabitNow's Extra goals): a measure that spans several Habits, such as cardio, would need a duplicate Habit.
- **OKR-style graded key results**: grading each period is the pass/fail scorecard the depressive-loop guard exists to avoid.
