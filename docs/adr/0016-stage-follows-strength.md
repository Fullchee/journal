# Stage follows Strength; a tree needs a yes

Stage used to be set by hand, so 193 of 200 Habits stayed seed and no reminder ever faded. Now seed, sprout and plant come from Strength (below 50%, 50–80%, 80% and up). Tree needs more: Strength of at least 95%, an age of at least 66 days, and a yes to "Do you do this without thinking?". Strength counts consistent repetition, but it can't tell whether a Habit feels automatic, and frequency tracks habit strength only in a stable context ([habit phases, reminder fading and decay](../research/habit-tracker-best-practices.md)). A tree stops ringing and leaves Today, following Stawarz 2015. It stays loggable, and a day without a log freezes its Strength rather than counting as a miss: logging every automatic Habit would be routine input. A tree falls back after a Rest mode, when its Season starts again, or on a Slipped tap. Changed context, not slow drift, is what breaks habits (Wood, Tam & Witt 2005).

## Considered options

- **Tree from Strength alone**: would promote on consistency the owner may still be forcing, and a hidden tree's unlogged days would drop it back within weeks.
- **Unlogged tree days count as misses**: makes demotion real, but every tree cycles back to ringing unless the owner logs it daily.
- **Keep Stage set by hand**: left 193 of 200 Habits at seed, so nothing faded.

## Consequences

- Only the tree decision is stored; seed, sprout and plant are read from History wherever Stage is shown.
- Sprout and plant change only the label until a follow-up thins reminders through the plant stage. No study has tested a fading schedule yet.
